Unit 9 · L14 · Search: BFS, DFS and A*

Every search is one rule for picking the next cell.

A castle guard has to walk from the gate to the tower, and its AI can only see the places right next to it. The frontier is every place it has discovered but not explored yet. BFS takes the oldest. DFS takes the newest. A* takes the lowest f = g + h. Follow the rule, click by click, and you are the guard's AI.
